# Permanent Strain of Granitic Rocks and Gabbro under Uniaxial Tension

Experimental dataset used in the publication:

> **Lin, W. & Takahashi, M.** (2026).
> *Occurrence of Permanent Strain of Granitic Rocks and Gabbro under Uniaxial Tension: Implicating Damages Undergone in Failure Process.*
> *Journal of the Society of Materials Science, Japan*, **75**(3), 179–185.
> https://doi.org/10.2472/jsms.2025.00102

Original citation in Japanese:

> 林為人・高橋学, 2026, 花崗岩質岩石と斑れい岩の一軸引張における永久ひずみの発生：破壊過程に被ったダメージを示す, 材料, **75**(3), 179–185. https://doi.org/10.2472/jsms.2025.00102

## Overview

Uniaxial tensile tests were performed on granitic rocks and gabbro specimens to
quantify their permanent strain behavior and relate it to damage accumulated
during the failure process. This repository provides the digital data of
stress–strain curves and summary tables of physical and mechanical properties
used in the publication above.

## Contents

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `data/stress_strain_curves.xlsx` | Stress–strain curves for each specimen |
| `data/tables_1_and_2.xls` | Summarized tables of physical and mechanical properties (Tables 1 & 2 of the paper) |

### Measured parameters

- Bulk density (dry) — g/cm³
- Porosity — %
- P-wave velocity, *V*ₚ — km/s
- Tensile strength — MPa
- Young's modulus — GPa
- Poisson's ratio — dimensionless
- Permanent strain in axial and lateral directions — 10⁻⁶
